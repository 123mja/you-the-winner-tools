🚀 Getting started

  1. Click Try Demo above to explore all the tools with no account — nothing you enter is saved.
  2. Ready to keep your data? Click Sign In and choose the Personal plan.
  3. Open Settings inside the app to turn tools on/off, adjust text size, and set up caregiver access.
  4. Each day, check the Plans or My Day tab — small daily use is what earns reward tokens.

❓ FAQ

Is my data private?
Yes. Your data lives on private Firebase servers and is visible only to you (and any caregiver you explicitly invite).

Does it work on my phone?
Yes — it runs in any mobile or desktop browser. No app install needed.

Can a caregiver and their loved one both use it?
Yes. Caregivers get their own settings and view; the person they support gets a simplified daily view.

What does it cost?
The demo is always free. Base is $7.99/month or $69/year and covers the everyday tools; Complete is $12.99/month or $119/year and adds the Wellness and Goals & Motivation packs. You can also add just one pack to Base instead of going Complete — see Plans above.
